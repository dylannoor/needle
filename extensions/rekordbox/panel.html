Must be inside your Music folder. Changes apply on the next download or restart.

Import in Rekordbox

  1. Open Preferences, then Advanced, then Database, and find "rekordbox xml".
  2. Set Imported Library to the XML file above.
  3. In the tree on the left, open "rekordbox xml" and find the Needle playlist. No "rekordbox xml" in the tree? Turn it on under Preferences, View, Layout.
  4. Drag its tracks into your Collection. Rekordbox reloads the file when you click the refresh icon next to "rekordbox xml".
